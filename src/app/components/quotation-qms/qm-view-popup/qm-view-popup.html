1. COMMENCEMENT:

This contract shall commence from 6 Aug 2026 and will continue unless terminated by either party in writing.

2. SCOPE OF CONTRACT:

By entering into any contract with the Transporter involving the carriage of goods, the Company agrees that:

A. the contract is a contract of carriage of goods by road if the carriage of the consignment actually takes place by road.

3. PROHIBITED GOODS & SECURITY:

3.1. The Transporter will not accept consignments that contain prohibited items.

3.2. The Company must ensure and certify by completing the Transporter's goods consignment note, or tendering the consignment to the Transporter, that the Company's consignment does not contain any articles prohibited by ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) and IATA (International Air Transport Association), or by other national or international regulations that govern aviation security. The Company must give a full description of the contents of the consignment in the consignment note, or other accompanying document. Further, the Company's responsibilities and liabilities are not extinguished by providing this information.

3.3. In case of carriage of goods by road, the Transporter will book the consignment on 'said to contain basis' and upon receipt of the goods, will issue the goods consignment note to the Company as per the information provided by the Company. The Transporter will not be liable to check the weight and other modalities.

3.4. In case of carriage of goods by air, consignments carried, or handled by the Transporter may be subject to security screening which could include the use of x-ray, explosive trace detection and other security screening methods; the Company must accept that its consignment may be opened and the contents of its consignment may be examined in transit.

3.5. The Transporter may be required to share information, including the Company's personal data for the consignment booked with the destination authorities for reason of legal compliances with statutory regulations of respective states.

4. RIGHT OF INSPECTION:

The Company agrees that the Transporter, or any government authorities, may, for security purposes, open and inspect the consignment booked by the Company at any time. In such cases, the Transporter shall intimate the Company about the situation in advance, or as soon as it comes to the knowledge of the Transporter. Further, the Transporter shall fully cooperate with the Company in providing any supporting documents or relevant information required by government authorities from time to time.

5. CALCULATION OF TRANSIT TIMES AND ROUTING OF CONSIGNMENTS:

Weekends, public holidays, bank holidays, or delays attributable to compliance with mandatory local statutory regulations, or security requirements, or other events beyond the Transporter's control are excluded when the Transporter quotes door-to-door delivery times in the Transporter's published literature. The route and the method by which the Transporter transports the Company's Consignment shall be at the sole discretion of the Transporter.

6. INCORRECT ADDRESS AND P.O. BOX NUMBERS:

If the Transporter is unable to deliver a consignment because an incorrect address has been supplied by the Company, the Transporter's staff/personnel will make all reasonable efforts to find the correct address. The Transporter will notify the Company of the correction and deliver or attempt to deliver the consignment to the correct address, although additional charges may apply, which shall be reimbursed by the Company. Deliveries to Post Office Box numbers are not accepted.

7. INABILITY TO DELIVER THE CONSIGNMENT:

7.1 Whenever the staff/personnel of the Transporter are unable to complete delivery of a consignment for whatever reason, the staff/personnel of the Transporter will try to leave a notice at the receiver's address stating that delivery has been attempted and informing the receiver of the whereabouts of the consignment. Further, whenever a receiver refuses to accept delivery, the staff/personnel of the Transporter will try to contact the Company and agree on the next appropriate action. In such cases, the Company agrees to reimburse the Transporter for any costs that may be incurred in forwarding, disposing of, or returning the consignment and charges (if any) paid by the Transporter for making a second or further delivery attempts, and for the agreed-on next appropriate action.
